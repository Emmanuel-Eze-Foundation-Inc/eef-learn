# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

EEF Learn exists so nobody has to learn alone. That only works if this community is safe, welcoming, and kind — to first-time contributors, to learners asking "basic" questions, and to people from every background.

## Standards

**Do:** be patient with newcomers; credit others' work; give feedback on the work, not the person; assume good faith; use welcoming language.

**Don't:** harass, demean, or discriminate; publish others' private information; sustain off-topic hostility; gatekeep ("just read the docs").

## Scope

Applies to all project spaces — issues, PRs, discussions, community calls, and events (physical and virtual).

## Enforcement

Report conduct issues to the Emmanuel Eze Foundation at conduct@emmanuelezefoundation.org. Reports are reviewed by the maintainers; outcomes range from a warning to a permanent ban. Reporters' privacy is protected.
